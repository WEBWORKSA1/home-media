Frequently asked questions

What is Home.Media?

An independent planning hub for home entertainment. We build free calculators and guides for screens, sound, streaming and media servers, and offer a free Home Media Plan that can connect you with vetted local installers if you want help.

Is the Home Media Plan really free?

Yes. You get a recommended setup and a cost range at no charge and with no obligation. If you ask to be matched with installers, partners may pay us a referral fee; that never changes the price you are quoted.

Do I have to hire an installer?

No. Many people use the plan to do it themselves. Choose 'DIY with a plan' and we'll focus on layouts, model shortlists and wiring tips instead of introductions.

How accurate are the cost estimates?

They are ranges built from published 2026 cost guides and typical gear prices. Real quotes depend on your room, region and finishes, which is why the plan step asks about them.

Which countries do you cover?

The tools work everywhere (feet or metres). Installer matching starts in the United States and Canada and expands as our partner network grows.
