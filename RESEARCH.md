# Home.Media — Concept, Revenue Model & 41-Site Benchmark

## The verdict

**Home.Media = the independent planning hub + lead marketplace for home entertainment.**
Plan it (free calculators), learn it (guides and videos), then get it built (free plan → matched with a vetted installer).

"Home media" is not just a phrase. It is an active **paid lead category**: home-services marketplaces price "home media installation" at roughly $72–$190/hour and run "Request a quote" flows for it (Homeyou). The average full home-theater install is about $20,000, with a typical $10,000–$60,000 range (Angi). An exact-match domain on a high-ticket service category is a lead-gen asset first and an AdSense site second.

## Why this beats the alternatives

| Option | Traffic potential | Revenue per visitor | Moat | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Streaming "what to watch" guide | Huge | Low (entertainment RPMs, no lead value) | None: JustWatch, Reelgood own it | Reject as core; keep as traffic feeder |
| Gear review site | High | Medium (affiliate) | Requires buying and testing hardware (RTINGS has tested 559 TVs) | Reject: capital-heavy, slow |
| Media-server (Plex/Jellyfin) tutorials | Medium | Low | Community already free (TRaSH, docs) | Feeder only |
| **Planning tools + installer lead marketplace** | Medium-high (calculators rank and get linked) | **High: leads on $1k–$60k jobs** | Tools + lead data + installer network | **Chosen** |

## Revenue model (planning assumptions, not guarantees)

Numbers below are assumptions to size the opportunity. Replace them with real data after 90 days.

| Stream | Mechanism | Assumption | Monthly at 30k visits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lead sales / referral fees | Free Home Media Plan wizard → sell or refer qualified leads to installers | 1.5% visit→lead, avg $60 realised per lead (TV-mount leads low, theater leads high) | 450 leads ≈ **$27,000** |
| Installer network subscriptions | Priority listing per territory (CEDIA and Control4 run dealer directories) | 20 pros × $149 | **$2,980** |
| AdSense | Auto ads + in-content units | ~60k pageviews × $10 RPM | **$600** |
| Sponsorship | "Tool sponsored by…" placements, newsletter | 2 sponsors × $750 | **$1,500** |
| Affiliate (phase 4) | Gear links from guides and tool results | — | upside |
| Donations | Pledges for operations, promos, hiring, prizes | small | <$200 |

**The flaw to fix early:** leads are worth $0 until someone buys them. The supply side (installers) must be recruited in parallel with traffic. The `pros.html` page captures installer sign-ups from day one; until a territory has a buyer, route leads to referral partners or do paid consultations.

**AdSense reality:** AdSense alone on a new site is a rounding error. It pays for hosting and validates traffic; the lead engine is the business.

## Trademark position

- **Home Media Magazine** was a trade publication (Questex) that ceased after its December 2017 issue; its team launched **Media Play News** in 2018. The site must never imply a connection to either.
- Brand is always written **Home.Media** (with the dot, as a domain name), never "HomeMedia" or "Home Media Magazine". No magazine framing, no "trade publication" claims, no awards named "Home Media Awards".
- Third-party marks (Dolby Atmos, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, Kodi, Netflix, HDMI, THX, IMAX, CEDIA, YouTube…) are used nominatively only. Full disclosure: `legal.html`.

## Feature map (what was built)

- **10 interactive tools:** Screen Size & Viewing Distance, Projector Throw & Brightness, Speaker Layout Planner (2.1→9.2.6), Streaming Cost & Cord-Cutting Savings, Bandwidth Planner, Media Server Storage, Home Theater Cost Estimator, Room Mode (bass) Calculator, HDMI Bandwidth Checker, Setup Matchmaker quiz.
- **Lead engine:** 5-step Free Home Media Plan wizard with instant estimate, lead scoring, UTM capture, multi-pro consent; service pages with cost ranges; sticky and inline CTAs in every tool and guide; installer network sign-up (supply side).
- **Content:** in-depth guides, self-updating news (daily), self-updating videos (daily, from creators' public feeds), FAQs.
- **Community and money:** Setup of the Month contest, donations/pledges, careers, advertise/sponsor, partnership owner bar on every page.
- **Compliance:** privacy (AdSense cookies + opt-out), terms, disclaimer, editorial policy, trademark & copyright disclosure, cookie notice, ads.txt, sitemap, robots.
