Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy anything?

No purchase is necessary to enter or win. A purchase does not improve your chances.

Who can enter?

Residents of the United States and Canada (excluding Quebec) who are 18 or older. Void where prohibited.

How is the winner chosen?

A judging panel scores entries on creativity, smart use of budget, and how helpful the tips are to other readers. It's a skill contest, not a random draw.
