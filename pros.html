Free Plan
For installers & integrators

Qualified home media leads, pre-scoped

Homeowners use our free tools to size screens, plan speaker layouts and set budgets before they ask for help. By the time a lead reaches you, the room, budget, timeline and scope are already on the page.

  1. Apply for your territory

    Tell us your services, service area and certifications. Membership is limited per area.

  2. Get scored leads

    Each lead includes project type, room size, budget band, timeline, ownership and tool results, tagged HOT, WARM or COOL.

  3. Win the job

    Contact the homeowner within one business day. We ask homeowners for feedback after each job.

What's in every lead

  • Project types (theater, mounting, Atmos, projector, whole-home audio, automation, network, media server)
  • Room type and size, existing equipment
  • Budget band and timeline
  • Own or rent, existing home, renovation or new build
  • ZIP/postal code and preferred contact method
  • Calculator results (screen size, speaker layout, cost estimate)
