Frequently asked questions

How many Mbps do I need for 4K streaming?

Netflix recommends 15 Mbps or more per 4K stream, 5 Mbps for 1080p and 3 Mbps for 720p. Other services are similar. Add those up for everything that might run at once, then leave headroom.

Why add 50% headroom?

Advertised speeds are best-case. Wi-Fi overhead, distance from the router, neighbours' networks and background updates all eat into it. 1.5× your peak keeps playback smooth.

How much data does 4K streaming use?

Roughly 7 GB per hour for 4K and about 3 GB per hour for HD. A household streaming four hours of 4K a day uses around 840 GB a month from viewing alone.

Is faster internet the fix for buffering?

Often it's Wi-Fi, not the plan. Move the router to a central spot, use a mesh system in large homes, and connect the main TV or streamer with Ethernet.
