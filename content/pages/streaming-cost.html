Five ways to cut your streaming bill

  1. Rotate. Keep two on-demand services at a time. Binge, cancel, switch.
  2. Use an antenna for local channels. In many areas, an antenna brings in major networks free. See our antenna guide.
  3. Take the ad tier where you don't mind ads. The gap between ad-supported and ad-free plans is often $8–$10 per month per service.
  4. Check bundles you already pay for. Phone carriers, internet providers and memberships often include a service.
  5. Add free apps. Tubi, Pluto TV and The Roku Channel fill a lot of evenings for $0.

Before you switch, make sure your internet can handle it with the bandwidth planner, and read how to cut the cord.
