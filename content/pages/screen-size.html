How the calculator works

Two industry guidelines frame the answer. The SMPTE recommendation is that the screen fills at least 30° of your horizontal field of view; any less and it feels small. THX recommends about 40° for the most cinematic seat. We use 35° as the sweet spot for mixed viewing, 40° for movies, 38° for gaming and 30° for news and sport.

The resolution figure answers a different question: how close do you have to sit to see every pixel? A person with 20/20 vision resolves detail about one arc-minute (1/60 of a degree) wide. Sit farther than that limit and a 4K screen looks as sharp as a 1080p one of the same size, though you still benefit from its HDR, colour and processing.

Rules of thumb

  • Living rooms: most people sit 8–10 ft (2.4–3 m) away. That points to 65–85 inch TVs today, bigger than many buyers expect.
  • Bedrooms: 6–8 ft away usually suits 50–65 inches.
  • Projectors: at 12 ft, a 105–120 inch 16:9 screen lands between 35° and the THX 40° mark. Check brightness with the projector calculator before you go big.
  • Scope (2.39:1) screens are wider and shorter; compare them by width or height, not diagonal.
