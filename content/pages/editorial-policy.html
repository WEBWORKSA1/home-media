Trust

Editorial policy

How we research, write and correct, and how we keep money away from recommendations.

Accuracy first

Our calculators use published standards and documented formulas (for example SMPTE and THX viewing angles, the 1-arc-minute visual-acuity limit, HDMI link rates and the room-mode equation). Cost figures name their source and date. Where we make an assumption, such as derating projector brightness by 30% for calibrated output, we say so on the page.

Original work

Every guide is written for Home.Media. We do not copy other publishers' text, and we do not publish fake reviews, invented testimonials or made-up statistics.

Independence

Advertisers, sponsors and installer partners have no say over our tools, guides or recommendations. Sponsored placements are labelled "Sponsored" or "Presented by". If we ever earn a commission from a product link, the page will say so.

Freshness

Guides show the date they were last updated. Prices that change often, such as streaming subscriptions, are editable in our tools and labelled as reference values. News and video sections refresh automatically every day from public sources, and link to the original publisher or creator.

Corrections

If something is wrong, tell us. We fix confirmed errors promptly and update the page date.
