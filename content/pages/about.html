About

Great picture and sound, planned properly

Most home-entertainment regret comes from three mistakes: the wrong screen size, speakers in the wrong places, and a budget that didn't match the room. Home.Media exists to prevent all three.

What we do

We build free, precise calculators (screen size, projector throw, speaker layout, streaming costs, bandwidth, media-server storage, room acoustics, HDMI and budgets) and write practical guides that get readers to a decision. When people want hands-on help, our free Home Media Plan gives them a recommended setup and estimate, and, only if they ask, introductions to vetted local installers.

How we're independent

  • We're not owned by a manufacturer, retailer or installer.
  • Sponsors and partners never edit our tools or recommendations, and sponsored content is labelled.
  • We show cost data with its source and date so you can check it.

How we make money

Advertising (Google AdSense), referral and membership fees from installers in our network, clearly labelled sponsorships, and support from readers. None of these change the numbers our tools produce.

Who writes this

The Home.Media Editorial Team: people who have mounted the TVs, pulled the wire, calibrated the subwoofers and run the media servers. Read our editorial policy for how we research, test and correct.

Get in touch

Questions, corrections or ideas for a new tool: contact us. Installers: join the network. Brands: advertise with us.
