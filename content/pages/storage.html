Storage is only half the build

For a family server, the CPU matters as much as the disks. If people will watch on phones, browsers or older TVs, the server may need to transcode (convert) video on the fly. Hardware transcoding (Intel Quick Sync on most recent Intel chips, or a supported GPU) handles several simultaneous 4K-to-1080p streams with little power. Direct play, where the TV plays the original file, needs almost no CPU at all.

Choosing software? Compare them in Plex vs Jellyfin vs Emby vs Kodi, then follow how to build a home media server. Only store media you own or are licensed to copy.
