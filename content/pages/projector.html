Reading the results

Throw distance is measured from the front of the lens to the screen. With a zoom lens you get a range: mount anywhere inside it and zoom to fill the screen. Image width is what the throw ratio cares about, so a wide 2.39:1 scope screen needs more distance than a 16:9 screen with the same height.

Foot-lamberts (fL) measure how bright the image looks from your seat. The formula is simple: lumens × screen gain ÷ screen area in square feet. That's why doubling the screen area halves the brightness. A small change in screen size has a big effect on how punchy HDR looks.
