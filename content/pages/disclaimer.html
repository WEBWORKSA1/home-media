Estimates are estimates

Calculator results and cost ranges are planning aids based on published standards, typical prices and stated assumptions. Real-world results depend on your room, equipment, eyesight, hearing, region and installer. Always confirm critical measurements on site.

Safety

Mounting heavy TVs, speakers and projectors, running cables in walls and adding power outlets can be dangerous and may be regulated. Use mounts rated for your equipment, fix into studs or appropriate anchors, follow electrical codes, and hire licensed professionals where required.

Streaming subscriptions, equipment and labour prices change often. Reference prices on this site are labelled with their basis and can be edited in our tools. Check current prices with the provider before you buy.
