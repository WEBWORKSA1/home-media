Privacy policy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Who we are

Home.Media ("we", "us") operates this website. You can reach us through our contact form.

Information you give us

When you submit a form (Home Media Plan, contact, newsletter, contest, careers, installer application, advertising enquiry or pledge), we receive the details you enter, such as your name, email, phone number, postcode and project details, plus the page you submitted from and how you arrived (for example a referring site or campaign tag). We use this to respond, deliver your plan, run contests and improve the site.

Installer introductions: only if you choose "Hire a pro" and tick the consent box, we share your project details and contact information with up to three independent installers so they can contact you with quotes. Installers are responsible for their own use of your information. We never sell your information to data brokers.

Forms are delivered by a third-party form-processing service (FormSubmit), which transmits submissions to our inbox.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our host and service providers log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages viewed. Our tools run in your browser; the numbers you type into calculators are not sent to us unless you submit a form. We store small preferences (theme, cookie notice, form progress) in your browser's local or session storage.

Cookies and advertising

We use Google AdSense to show ads. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message for advertising cookies, provided through Google's certified consent management platform, where required.

Embedded content

Videos are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode and load only when you press play. Once you play a video, YouTube may set cookies under its own privacy policy. News headlines link to the original publishers' sites.

How long we keep information

We keep form submissions for as long as needed to respond and for reasonable record-keeping (generally no more than 24 months), unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent to installer introductions or newsletters, at any time via our contact form (choose "Privacy request"). Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA or Canada's PIPEDA) you may have additional rights, which we honour.

Children

This site is for adults planning home entertainment. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA).

Changes

We'll update this page when our practices change and revise the date above.
