Frequently asked questions

What happens after I submit?

A specialist reviews your answers and replies within one business day with your plan: recommended screen size, speaker layout, a phased budget and model shortlists. If you asked for installers, we introduce up to three vetted local pros so you can compare quotes.

Will I be spammed with calls?

No. You choose how you want to be contacted. We introduce a maximum of three installers, only if you ask, and never sell your details to data brokers.

How does Home.Media make money if the plan is free?

Installers in our network may pay a referral or membership fee, and the site shows ads. Neither changes your quote, and you're never obliged to hire anyone.

Can I use the plan to do it myself?

Absolutely. Choose 'DIY with a plan' and we'll focus on layout, parts and wiring guidance.
